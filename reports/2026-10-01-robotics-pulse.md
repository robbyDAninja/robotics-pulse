# Robotics Pulse — Sep 17 – Oct 01, 2026

## Top Signal
Boston Dynamics' redesigned humanoid hand—dropping the pinkie and ring finger in favor of a three-finger-plus-thumb design—signals a major shift in dexterous robotics philosophy: anthropomorphic doesn't mean human-identical. The team's user-research approach (literally taping engineers' fingers to understand constraints) demonstrates how real-world task analysis beats biomimicry dogma. This matters because it suggests humanoid robotics is entering a pragmatic optimization phase where form follows function rather than aesthetic parity with humans, potentially unlocking faster deployment in industrial settings. (The Robot Report, IEEE Spectrum Robotics)

## Developments

- **Maven Robotics secures $100M for mobile manipulator production** — The startup is moving from research to mass manufacturing of task-specific mobile manipulation systems for industrial automation. This represents concrete capital betting on the near-term viability of autonomous task execution in warehouses and factories. (The Robot Report)

- **Sensing infrastructure emerges as critical bottleneck** — Vishay Precision Group and ForceN are highlighting force/torque and custom sensing as essential capabilities for humanoid deployment in real environments. Accurate physical feedback is becoming the unglamorous but essential layer between robot control and successful manipulation—a shift from pure AI focus to hardware-software co-design. (The Robot Report)

- **Safety-by-design frameworks face input validation crisis** — Research reveals that robot safety functions can work perfectly while acting on manipulated or false sensor data, exposing a critical gap in threat modeling. This matters for your AI agent systems: bulletproof logic gates won't save you from adversarial inputs. (The Robot Report)

- **Humanoid skill acquisition moving from labs to production data** — Innodata's new motion-capture lab and Intrinsic's open-source ROS-compatible platform reflect broader industry consolidation around sim-to-real training pipelines for humanoid movement and task learning. The infrastructure for scaling humanoid deployment is being standardized. (The Robot Report, Robohub)

- **Destro AI reframes robot-human coordination as software, not robotics** — A non-robotics company building the coordination layer between robots and workers suggests the real bottleneck isn't mechanical—it's orchestration and human-in-the-loop integration. (TechCrunch)

## Figure Watch
Figure AI continues wrestling with commercialization constraints: decommissioning of F.02 units and significant infrastructure investments (the San Jose campus sale for $260M+) indicate the company is restructuring post-containment incident. Research on robot scaling laws remains episodic and incomplete, suggesting Figure's path to production-ready systems remains uncertain despite earlier hype. (Figure AI Watch)

## Trend Line
Robotics is consolidating around *operational infrastructure*—sensing, simulation, coordination software, and production pipelines—rather than pursuing incremental improvements to robot morphology or isolated AI capabilities.
